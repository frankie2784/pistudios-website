Truth. Beauty. Code.
Melbourne·Australia
I work where analysis meets craft: building tools and products that treat rigour and elegance as the same requirement, not competing ones. My background flows between spatial analytics, complex modelling, applied AI, strategy and decision support across a range of industries, but the throughline isn't any particular dataset or platform; it's a habit of taking something complicated, unearthing the structure and disorder within it, and building atop tools and processes that can untie knots, extract buried value, reduce effort and help solve real-world challenges.
Pi Studios is where this habit comes home to roost: a pipeline hand-built, a workflow automated, a dataset made simple and legible at a glance. I build in iterations, not manifestos, testing an idea in code faster than most people can finish describing it, then refining until it's right. That's the tinkering instinct put to work: fast, focused and aimed squarely at a finished result.
Truth, Beauty, Code. That's not a slogan so much as a description of how I see the world. Truth in the sense of getting the underlying logic of the problem and solution right. Beauty in the sense of caring how it looks and feels once it's built. Code as the medium where, for me, those two things stop being separate concerns and become the same discipline.
